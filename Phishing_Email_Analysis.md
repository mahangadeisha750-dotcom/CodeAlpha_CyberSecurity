Phishing Email Analysis

1. Objective

To understand how to identify a phishing email and learn how to stay safe from online fraud.

2. Sample Phishing Email

From: support@bank-security-example.com

Subject: Urgent! Your bank account will be blocked!

Message:

Dear Customer,

Your bank account will be blocked today. Click the link below and enter your password and OTP to verify your account.

Verify your account now

Note: This is a fictional email created for educational purposes.

3. Warning Signs

1. The email creates fear and urgency.
2. The sender's email address looks suspicious.
3. It asks for a password and OTP.
4. It contains an unfamiliar link.

4. Analysis

This email may be a phishing attempt because it tries to create panic and asks for confidential banking information.

5. Safety Measures

- Never share your password or OTP.
- Do not click unknown links.
- Verify messages through the official bank app or website.
- Report suspicious emails.

6. Conclusion

This activity teaches us how to identify suspicious emails and protect ourselves from phishing attacks.
